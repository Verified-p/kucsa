About KUCSA

A community built around technology

The Kisii University Computing Students Association (KUCSA) brings together students passionate about computing and technology, creating opportunities for learning, networking, innovation and professional development.

Whether you are exploring your first programming language, developing advanced software solutions, participating in technical events, or preparing for your professional career, KUCSA provides a community where computing students can connect, collaborate and grow.
