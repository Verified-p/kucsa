How attendance works
  • A new session starts as Draft.
  • A management user opens the session when attendance should begin.
  • Members can mark themselves present while the session is open.
  • Each member receives an attendance record for the session.
  • Management can review and correct attendance records.
  • The session can be closed manually or become expired after its attendance window.
