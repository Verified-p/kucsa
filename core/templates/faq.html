Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about KUCSA and the Digital Computing Community Platform.

Any student enrolled in a computing-related programme at Kisii University is welcome to join KUCSA by creating an account, applying for membership, and completing the membership registration process.

Register an account, submit your membership application, upload proof of payment, and wait for verification by the KUCSA executive team. Once approved, you will gain full access to member features.

Members can view announcements, register for events, monitor attendance, update their profiles, and participate in KUCSA activities through the platform.

Your dashboard will display your membership status. You may also receive a notification once your membership has been verified by the executive team.

Click the Forgot Password link on the login page and follow the instructions to reset your password.

You can visit the Contact page and send a message to the KUCSA executive team, or reach them using the contact information provided.
